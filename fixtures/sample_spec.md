# Conduit sample application

Conduit is a lightweight blogging app. Visitors can browse and paginate the global feed. Registered users can sign in, publish articles with a title, description, body, and tags, favorite articles, comment, follow authors, and update their profile. A favorite click changes the count exactly once. Paginated feeds must not repeat articles. Article titles and account email addresses are validated. Optional analytics failures must not undo a successful user action.
